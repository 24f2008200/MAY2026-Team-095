# SCRUM Meetings Schedule and Minutes

**Meeting Mode:** Google Meet  
**Meeting Frequency:** Scheduled evening sessions

---

## SCRUM Meeting – June 20, 2026

- Team members introduced themselves and discussed their individual roles and areas of expertise.
- Multiple project domains were analyzed, and the **Apartment Association Issue Tracking System** was finalized as the project.
- The overall workflow of the issue tracking system was brainstormed.
- Key pain points were identified, including:
  - Delayed issue resolution
  - Lack of transparency
  - Ineffective communication
  - Absence of accountability

---

## SCRUM Meeting – June 24, 2026

- Team progress was reviewed, and individual responsibilities were clarified.
- Compliance requirements, including audio and video consent for user interviews, were discussed.
- The overall system architecture and design approach were reviewed.
- SMART guidelines for writing effective user stories were introduced and discussed.
- Primary, secondary, and tertiary users of the system were identified and finalized.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – June 26, 2026

- Interview findings were analyzed, and key user pain points were documented.
- Seventeen SMART user stories covering the complete application workflow were drafted.
- Documentation and user stories underwent a sanity check for completeness and consistency.
- Milestone 2 was initiated, focusing on frontend wireframing and user interface design.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – July 1, 2026

- The project technology stack was finalized, including:
  - Flask
  - Vue.js
  - Tailwind CSS
  - Supporting technologies
- UI wireframes were reviewed and validated.
- Initial UI designs for the development phase were proposed and discussed.
- The project workflow timeline and sprint schedule were finalized.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – July 3, 2026

- The frontend template was presented and reviewed by the team.
- The Jira project structure, task tracking process, and workflow were discussed.
- Best practices for maintaining the GitHub repository, including version control and collaboration, were established.
- The team agreed to proceed with frontend and backend development in parallel.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – July 8, 2026

- API requirements for communication between the frontend and backend were discussed.
- The distinction between the class diagram and the database design (ER) diagram was clarified.
- The current progress of the frontend implementation was reviewed.
- The frontend folder structure was discussed, including the organization of:
  - Components
  - Pages
  - Services
  - Assets
  - Utility files
- The agreed folder structure will be followed to improve maintainability and scalability.
