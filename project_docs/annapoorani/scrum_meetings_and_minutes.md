# SCRUM Meetings Schedule and Minutes

**Meeting Mode:** Google Meet  
**Meeting Frequency:** Scheduled evening sessions

---

## SCRUM Meeting – June 20, 2026

- Team members introduced themselves and discussed their individual roles and areas of expertise.
- Multiple project domains were analyzed, and the **Apartment Association Issue Tracking System** was finalized as the project.
- The overall workflow of the issue tracking system was brainstormed.
- Key pain points were identified, including:
  - Delayed issue resolution
  - Lack of transparency
  - Ineffective communication
  - Absence of accountability

---

## SCRUM Meeting – June 24, 2026

- Team progress was reviewed, and individual responsibilities were clarified.
- Compliance requirements, including audio and video consent for user interviews, were discussed.
- The overall system architecture and design approach were reviewed.
- SMART guidelines for writing effective user stories were introduced and discussed.
- Primary, secondary, and tertiary users of the system were identified and finalized.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – June 26, 2026

- Interview findings were analyzed, and key user pain points were documented.
- Seventeen SMART user stories covering the complete application workflow were drafted.
- Documentation and user stories underwent a sanity check for completeness and consistency.
- Milestone 2 was initiated, focusing on frontend wireframing and user interface design.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – July 1, 2026

- The project technology stack was finalized, including:
  - Flask
  - Vue.js
  - Tailwind CSS
  - Supporting technologies
- UI wireframes were reviewed and validated.
- Initial UI designs for the development phase were proposed and discussed.
- The project workflow timeline and sprint schedule were finalized.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – July 3, 2026

- The frontend template was presented and reviewed by the team.
- The Jira project structure, task tracking process, and workflow were discussed.
- Best practices for maintaining the GitHub repository, including version control and collaboration, were established.
- The team agreed to proceed with frontend and backend development in parallel.

---

## SCRUM Meeting – July 8, 2026

- API requirements for communication between the frontend and backend were discussed.
- The distinction between the class diagram and the database design (ER) diagram was clarified.
- The current progress of the frontend implementation was reviewed.
- The frontend folder structure was discussed, including the organization of:
  - Components
  - Pages
  - Services
  - Assets
  - Utility files
- The agreed folder structure will be followed to improve maintainability and scalability.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - July 10, 2026

- A few changes were made to the frontend.
- The GitHub repository was well organized to accommodate the related project components.
- A prototype of the class diagram was prepared by the backend team.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - July 11, 2026

- Demonstrated the current frontend user interface to the client and explained the implemented features and navigation flow.
- Answered the client's questions regarding the frontend design, usability, and planned functionality.
- Discussed the approach for preparing test cases for the APIs to be developed in upcoming sprints.
- Collected feedback and suggestions from the client for improving the user interface, functionality, and overall user experience.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - July 12, 2026

- The database design diagram was prepared by the backend team.
- A draft of the Milestone 2 deliverables document was prepared.
- The team decided to use Neon as the database platform.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - July 22, 2026

- A decision was taken to continue with PostgreSQL instead of SQLite.
- Requirements for the Milestone submission were collected and checked for any pending items.
- A few frontend UI changes were discussed.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - July 26, 2026

- API testing using Swagger was discussed.
- A few unexpected results were identified and discussed.
- It was decided to implement a **Request Access** feature for residents to ensure that users cannot directly log in without the required access approval.
- A few previously identified errors were resolved.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - July 29, 2026

- Extensive API testing was carried out.
- The testing team started working on **Pytest** for automated API testing.
- The backend team decided to focus on further development and refinement of the **Staff APIs**.
- The issue with attachment uploading was still unresolved and was identified as an area requiring further attention.
- It was decided to include links to the **raw JSON results, backend code, Pytest code, and Swagger-compatible YAML file** in the Milestone 3 deliverables PDF.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - August 7, 2026

- The team decided to start working on **Analytics** for the Admin Dashboard.
- The **Notifications** page was integrated for all three user roles.
- Several improvements were made to both the frontend and backend.
- The team developed an approach to address and implement the **four user feedback points**.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - August 9, 2026

- The possibility of rescheduling the final presentation slot with a time change on the same day was discussed.
- A few additional user feedback points collected during Milestone 3 were reviewed, and the team planned to work on them as well.
- The backend and testing teams focused on completing the remaining testing activities and Milestone 4 submission.
- Doubts and clarifications regarding Milestone 4 were discussed.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - August 19, 2026

- The application was showcased based on the features developed so far, and the special features implemented were explained to the client.
- The application was reviewed thoroughly to identify any required changes or improvements.
- A few UI and implementation enhancements were identified and discussed.
- The team decided to clean the existing database and use a fresh database to remove test data and other unnecessary or obsolete records accumulated during development and testing.

---

## SCRUM Meeting - August 21, 2026

- The team decided to enhance and upgrade the **Notifications** page.
- Most of the **Milestone 5 requirements** were collected and reviewed to identify any pending deliverables.
- After completing the required changes, the team decided to run the **Pytest test suite** and submit the required deliverables for Milestone 5.
